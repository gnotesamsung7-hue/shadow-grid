Codex: the world of the Shadow Grid

Lumina City

A harbor city that never quite sleeps. Every night at exactly 3:00 a.m., a silver fog called the Veil rolls in from the river and stays for three minutes. Clocks stop, phones go dark, and in the morning nobody remembers a thing. People call it the Hollow Hour.

The Veil

Inside the Veil, nothing has a face until it is touched. That is why every unit is a shadow until it clashes, and why whole squads vanish in the forests, where the fog runs thickest. From high ground the fog thins enough to make out shapes.

The Shadow Grid

Inside the Veil, the streets of Lumina City fold into a field of hexagons that shifts every night. The Night Division calls it the Shadow Grid. Whoever controls the Grid when the Veil lifts controls what the city remembers.

Night Division

A secret police unit that fights whatever comes through the Veil. On the streets, its spirit-gun officers hunt ghouls one alley at a time. On the battlefield, its tactical squad fights the larger war, directed over comms by Field Director Mark Dave.

The Rose Legion

When the Veil touches a mirror, it can copy whoever is reflected in it. The Rose Legion is an army of these copies, called Echoes: perfect doubles of the Night Division squad, wearing their faces in rose red. They fight exactly as well as the people they were copied from.

The President's anchor

President Aoi refused to leave the city. Her will anchors Lumina City to reality. When an Echo of the President shatters, every Echo loyal to her loses its shape. If the real Aoi is taken, the city wakes up on the wrong side of the mirror.

Lantern crates and the lumen barrage

Spirit officers drop lantern crates through the fog with supplies: Recon, Smoke or a Rally order. At two minutes in, the city's lighthouses fire lumen shells that burn the Veil away from the edges inward. Anything still standing at the edges burns with it. At 3:03 the Veil lifts, and whoever holds more of the field holds the night.

The squad

Kaede, Armor. Hot-headed tank ace who named her tank Biscuit and talks to it more than to people.

Haru, Infantry. First-year rifleman, too honest to bluff, so he trusts everyone else to do it for him.

Mio, Anti-Tank. Rocket lancer and Kaede's rival since academy. Her launcher has more stickers than paint.

Sora, Commander. The silver-haired marshal has never lost a duel to a soldier. She only fears what she cannot see.

Kage, Spy. Nobody in the Division has seen Kage's face. Strikes once, from the dark, then disappears.

President Aoi, VIP. The youngest president in the city's history, and the calmest person in any room.

How to play

Each turn, move one unit to a neighbouring hex. Moving onto an enemy starts a clash: both units are revealed and the loser is removed. The winner stays exposed to the enemy.

Echoes look like hooded tokens until you learn what they are. Units hiding in a forest are invisible until something bumps into them.

Terrain: water blocks movement. In the city, defenders win ties, and defending Infantry beats Armor. Armor can't enter forests but can dash 2 hexes across open plains (no attack on a dash). A unit on a hill senses the type of enemies next to it.

The President can't attack, except the enemy President, and can't move two turns in a row.

Lantern crates drop at 0:45 and 1:30: Recon reveals an enemy, Smoke hides one of your exposed units, Rally gives an extra move. At 2:00 the lumen barrage hits the outer columns, at 2:30 the back rows. Any unit ending its turn there is destroyed. When the Veil lifts at 3:00, the side with more units wins.
