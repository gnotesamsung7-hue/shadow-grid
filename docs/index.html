Hidden squads on a hex battlefield. Find the enemy President in three minutes.

Your squad

Armor beats Infantry. Infantry beats Anti-Tank. Anti-Tank beats Armor. Same type, both fall. The Commander beats every troop, but a Spy wins any fight it starts.

Battlefield

Shot clock

How to play

Each turn, move one piece to a neighbouring hex. Moving onto an enemy starts a clash: both units are revealed and the loser is removed. The winner stays exposed to the enemy.

Enemy units look like hooded tokens until you learn what they are. Units hiding in a forest are invisible until something bumps into them.

Terrain: water blocks movement. In the city, defenders win ties, and defending Infantry beats Armor. Armor can't enter forests but can dash 2 hexes across open plains (no attack on a dash). A unit on a hill senses the type of enemies next to it.

The VIP can't attack, except the enemy VIP, and can't move two turns in a row.

Supply crates drop at 0:45 and 1:30: Recon reveals an enemy, Smoke hides one of your exposed units, Rally gives an extra move. At 2:00 artillery hits the outer columns, at 2:30 the back rows. Any unit ending its turn there is destroyed. At 3:00 the side with more units wins.
